What to Expect

First off, thank you for considering traveling to celebrate this important milestone with us. Our goal is to create a rich experience, for you and for us!

Skiing and mountains are what brings us joy. The mountain culture of creating comfort and access (huts, lifts, hotels, bars) within big, wild mountains is something that is unique to the Alps - and we want to create an excuse to share this special world with you. Plus, the Aprés!

The days will have a general flow of chatting over coffee in the morning, groups skiing and exploring the mountains during the day, getting spritzes for Apres, and having dinner and celebrations at the hotel in the evening. There are a few wedding-specific events we will carve time out for (see itinerary below).

We so look forward to spending this time with you, skiing, eating, drinking and celebrating our love.

🏠

The Hotel

We will be staying at the Berghotel Jochelius and have the entire hotel reserved from January 24-28, 2027. The hotel is on mountain and not accessible by vehicle - more on arrival logistics below.

Each couple will have their own room, and single guests will either have their own room or be grouped together with friends you already know and love. All rooms have ensuite bathrooms and should have all the comforts we need for a relaxing stay.

The Food & Drinks

The hotel will provide breakfast, lunch, afternoon snacks, and dinner each day. They can accommodate any dietary restrictions if we know about them ahead of time.

The food will mostly consist of regional fare such as Kaiserschmarn, Spätzle, Rösti. Though they also apparently have a wood fired pizza oven!

The hotel has a fully stocked and staffed cafe and bar. Coffee, tea, flavored water is available at all times and included.

We will be covering the bar tab the nights of the ceremony & party.

🎒

What to Bring

  • Personal ski gear (Backcountry ski gear, if you have it)
  • Swimsuit (there is a sauna)

Towels and "Hausschuhe" (german for house slippers) are provided so you don't need to bring those.

The Skiing

Nauders is a smaller resort right at the corner of the Austria-Italy-Swiss border. It consists of plenty of in-bounds (piste) skiing, as well as access to backcountry terrain. The hotel is located about mid-mountain, and will provide convenient access to the lifts at the beginning and end of each day.

Learn more about Nauders →

Lift Tickets: Tickets are available online around September for about half price. We will send a reminder email to everyone, when they become available. You can expect them to be about 40 euro per day.

For the arrival day, if you do not plan to ski and you are only riding the lifts to get to the hotel, the ticket office sells a special ticket for hotel guests for about 10 euro.

Travel

Everything you need know to get to Nauders

Arrival Logistics

Guests can arrive any time of the day on January 24. The Jochelius has a designated area at the base of the ski lifts to drop your personal bags which will be delivered directly to the hotel. To reach the Jochelius hotel, you will ride up the Bergastelseilbahn (name of the gondola lift), and then ski down Piste 11 (see ski map).

Air Travel

🚨 New in 2027: US passport holders will need to obtain an ETIAS (European travel information and authorization system) before your trip. The cost is 20 euros. More Info Here →

The closest international airports are Zurich, Switzerland or Munich, Germany. Both airports have direct flights from major US cities, including Denver.

While Innsbruck, Austria is geographically closer to Nauders, it is a small airport and will require multiple layovers.

Please ask questions as needed for clarity.

🚗

Travel to Nauders by Car

The easiest way to travel to Nauders is by renting a car. Car travel from both Zurich and Munich is approximately 3 hours.

There is designated parking at the base of the ski lifts to leave vehicles during your time at the hotel.

🚆

By Train & Bus

Traveling to Nauders is also possible via train and bus. The Swiss and German rail systems have trains running from Munich and Zurich to the town of Landeck, Austria. Once in Landeck, there is a bus that will take you to Nauders.

The bus runs approximately every hour.

  • Train/bus station in Landeck: Landeck-Zams
  • Bus station in Nauders: Nauders Mühle

FAQ

What is the dress code?

One nice outfit for the evening of the ceremony - there is no strict dress code, just don't show up in your ski underwear. For the party night, whatever makes you feel comfortable to shake and pop all night long.

Can I bring a plus one?

We've done our best to accommodate plus ones. Due to the hotel's capacity, we have had to keep the guest list to close friends and family. If you have a plust one, they are already included as apart of your invitation/rsvp.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

The hotel can accommodate any dietary restrictions if we know about them ahead of time. Please note them in your RSVP.

How do I get to the hotel once I'm in Nauders?

The Jochelius has a designated area at the base of the ski lifts to drop your personal bags which will be delivered directly to the hotel. To reach the hotel, you will ride up the Bergastelseilbahn, and then ski down Piste 11. We will provide a detailed map on this website as we get closer to the event.

Can I leave the hotel in the evening?

Due to the hotel's mid-mountain location, you can go in and out of town during the day while the ski lifts are running, but there is no convenient access once the lifts shut down for the day. If an emergency arises, we will be able to get the hotel to operate their snow cat to take you into town, but otherwise please plan around this.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by July 1, 2026 so we can finalize our headcount.

What about wedding gifts?

We appreciate your generosity, but your presence is all we ask of you! Please don't give us a gift other than that.

What do I do if I have to leave early or arrive late?

We understand that we all have busy lives and that unusual circumstances may come up, but we do ask you to make a good effort to commit to the duration of the event. If something unavoidable prevents you from this, please note that due to the package we have with the hotel we are unable to pro-rate the per person cost for fewer nights. Please let us know ahead of time if you plan to stay less than 4 nights.

Do I need a lift ticket just to get to the hotel on arrival day?

For the arrival day, if you do not plan to ski and you are only riding the lifts to get to the hotel, the ticket office sells a special ticket for hotel guests for about 10 euro.
